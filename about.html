Black Side

About Black Side

Black Side is a free arcade for your phone. You sign in, pick a game and try to beat your best score.

The games

  • Memory Match: find all the pairs in as few moves as you can.
  • Tap Rush: tap as fast as you can for 10 seconds.
  • Tic-Tac-Toe: play against the computer.
  • Colour Match: tap the colour a word is written in, not the colour it names.
  • Math Rush: solve quick sums against the clock.
  • Boom Blast: tap the gems and dodge the bombs.

Practice coins

Every game uses free practice coins. They have no cash value, you cannot buy them, and they cannot be exchanged for money, prizes or anything else. The site never takes real payments.

Your account

You can sign in with email or Google. Your scores and coins are stored on your own device. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
