Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What we collect

When you create an account or sign in with Google, we receive your email address, your name and your profile photo if you have one. This is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.

What stays on your device

Your practice coins, best scores and the demo payment entries you add (card brand, last 4 digits and expiry, or a UPI ID) are saved in your browser storage on your own device. We never save a full card number or CVV, and no real payment is ever made.

Cookies and advertising

This site shows ads from third-party advertising partners, and may use Google AdSense. These partners may use cookies or similar technology to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage personalised ads at adssettings.google.com, or by clearing cookies in your browser.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. It is processed only by the services needed to run the site, such as Google Firebase and our advertising partners.

Children

Black Side is not meant for children under 13.

Your choices

To ask us to delete your account or data, email us at the address on the Contact page.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
